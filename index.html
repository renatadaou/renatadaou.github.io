About me

Renata Daou

Substack · LinkedIn · GitHub · rcdaou@gmail.com

Digital marketing · data journalism · social media · EN / PT-BR / ES · Brazil · working worldwide

My work is data-driven from start to finish, including audience research, content strategy, KPI tracking and performance analysis, in English, Portuguese and Spanish.

Data journalism. At Bloomberg News, I've covered climate, energy and commodities, from record coffee prices to geothermal energy. At The Connecticut Mirror, I turned public data into stories about elections, cost of living, immigration and lead pipes. I hold a Master of Science in Data Journalism from Columbia University.

Social media and digital marketing. I've run Instagram accounts for Malala Fund's Assembly and Penn State Global, and I create UGC-style video. At Penn State Global, I supported communications and events across 20 campuses for 10,000+ international students and researchers, including the study abroad fair, where I placed booths for colleges, partner universities and vendors from around the world. I also write my own travel and culture newsletter, Postcards and Plot Twists (see the case study at the end of this page). I'm building on that with a postgraduate degree in Marketing and Digital Media at Fundação Getulio Vargas (FGV).
